Why Brigade Stellaris

Designed for lives that outgrow expectations

01

Low-Density Luxury

Just 284 residences across four towers on 5.19 acres — six homes per floor, no shared walls, and roughly 75% of the land kept open and green.

02

Address of Access

Phoenix Marketcity and Palladium next door, Velachery MRTS minutes away, and Guindy, OMR and the airport within easy reach.

03

Light, Air & Orientation

Around 90% of homes face North or East, with double-glazed windows, wide decks and VRV air-conditioning across living and bedrooms.

04

Built Responsibly

Solar-powered common areas, rainwater harvesting, EV charging for every apartment and Brigade's Net Zero Carbon 2045 roadmap.
